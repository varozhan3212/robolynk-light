Flash RoboLynk Lite

Installs the firmware straight from this page. Nothing to download.

This browser cannot talk to serial devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a desktop.

Serial access is blocked. The page must be served over HTTPS.

  1. Connect the board by USB-C.
  2. Press Connect and pick the board's serial port.
  3. Choose Install and let it finish — about 70 seconds.
When it finishes, unplug the board and plug it back in. The flasher reports a reset, but on this chip that does not actually restart the application. Without a physical replug the screen stays dark and it looks as though the flash failed.

After flashing

  1. The robot shows TAP TO BEGIN. Tap and accept the terms.
  2. Choose Phone Setup, join the RoboLynk-XXXX network from your phone, and open 192.168.4.1.
  3. Pick your Wi-Fi from the list. Only 2.4 GHz networks appear — the robot cannot use 5 GHz.
  4. Open Settings → Info and note the six-character Setup Code.
  5. Create an account, then add the robot with that code.
